Testing guide
for the QA team

What Signal Shala is, what the app does, and how to test it step by step on a phone and a computer.

In this folder: this guide · Signal-School-Test-Cases.xlsx (the list of tests) · test-files (photos and sheets to upload).

1What is Signal Shala?

A school for children who live and work at traffic signals.

In big cities many children spend the whole day at traffic signals with their families – selling flowers, toys or books between the cars. Most of them have never been to school. Signal Shala (“signal school”) started in 2016 right under the Teen Hath Naka flyover in Thane, Maharashtra, in classrooms made from shipping containers. It is run by the trust Samarth Bharat Vyaspeeth together with the Thane Municipal Corporation.

The school gives these children lessons, a meal, health check-ups and a safe place, and helps them move on to regular schools.

Why this matters for testing

  • The teachers use cheap Android phones, often with weak internet. The app must be simple, big and quick.
  • Many children join and leave during the year (families move). Admitting, leaving and re-admitting must be easy.
  • Attendance is taken every morning. It is the most important screen – it must never lose data.
  • Users speak Marathi, Hindi, Gujarati or English.

2What does the app do?

Every day

  • Attendance in a few taps (works without internet too)
  • Children’s records: photo, parents, phone, health
  • Diary: notes about a child or the whole class
  • Syllabus: tick topics as they are taught
  • Dashboard for the principal: who is absent, who needs a call

Now and then

  • Marks and printable report cards
  • Import many children from Excel
  • Leaving certificate when a child leaves
  • New academic year: move children up a class
  • Looks: 6 colour styles for the whole school

5A quick tour

Login – choose the language, then mobile number or email and password.
Today (teacher) – the class, a big “Take attendance” button and syllabus progress.
Take attendance – everyone starts Present; tap A or L, then “Save attendance”.
Child’s profile – photo, class, parent with call and WhatsApp, tabs for diary, past years, report card, health.
Dashboard (principal) – today’s numbers and children absent many days in a row.
Look – the principal tries a look (here “Blackboard”) before using it for everyone.

On a phone the menu is the bar at the bottom: Today/Dashboard, Attendance, Students, Syllabus, More. On a computer it is on the left side.

6How to test

Before you start

  • An Android phone with Chrome, and a computer with Chrome. (A second phone helps for the “Looks” tests.)
  • The app link you received. Open it in Chrome.
  • Copy the folder test-files to the phone (e.g. through Google Drive or a USB cable) so you can upload photos and sheets from the phone.
  • Open Signal-School-Test-Cases.xlsx (Excel or Google Sheets). Read the sheet “Start here”.

For every test case

1
Read the row: Who logs in, Device, Where. Log in as that person (passwords are on the sheet “Logins”).
2
Do the Steps exactly, with the Test data given.
3
Compare with the Expected result. Look at the whole screen, not only the one thing.
4
Choose a Status: Pass Fail Blocked Skipped (it starts as Not run). Write the date and your name.
5
Anything strange? Write it in Comments, even if the test passed. For a Fail, also fill a row in the sheet “Bugs”.

On every screen, also check

  • All text can be read; nothing is cut off or overlapping
  • No sideways scrolling on a phone
  • Buttons are easy to tap with a thumb
  • The bottom bar never hides a button
  • Messages are clear and in the chosen language
  • Nothing keeps “loading” for more than a few seconds
  • After saving, the change is still there when you reopen
  • Photos and names match the right child

Test in this order

DaySheet “Test cases”, rows starting with…
Day 1LG (Login) and TC (Teacher) – on a phone
Day 2CL (Office clerk) and PR (Principal) – computer and phone
Day 3OW (Trust owner), LK (Looks) and GN (General) – then re-check every Fail

Screenshot on Android: press Power + Volume down together. Also write the time when it happened.

Please do NOT

  • Make the new year 2027-28 “current” (test PR-20). Keep it “Planned”, or all classes change for everyone.
  • Add a holiday for today, or change the school’s weekly holidays – attendance tests would stop working.
  • Use real children’s names, photos or phone numbers.
  • Forget to set the look back to “Signal Shala” at the end (test LK-12).

Something is stuck?

  • Page looks old or empty – pull down to reload, or close and open the tab.
  • “Too many wrong attempts” – that login waits 15 minutes. Use another login meanwhile.
  • “Too many attempts. Please wait a moment.” – too many logins from your network in a short time. Wait a few minutes; tell the developer if it keeps happening.
  • The test data is mixed up (e.g. a child is already “left”) – ask for a test data reset. It brings back everything as described in this guide (your changes are removed).
  • A test depends on an earlier one that failed – mark it Blocked and say which one in Comments.
